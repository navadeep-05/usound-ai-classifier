    🧠 Model Details

    The classifier is powered by a ResNet‑18 convolutional neural network pretrained on ImageNet and fine‑tuned on the UrbanSound8K dataset. The model was trained for 30 epochs with a cross‑entropy loss and Adam optimizer, achieving over 87 % validation accuracy. During inference, the uploaded audio is transformed into a mel‑spectrogram, fed into the network, and the class with the highest softmax probability is returned.
